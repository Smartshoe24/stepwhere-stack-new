Night Trail

An operations console for the people who keep every shoe reporting. Near-black and quiet by default, so the only things that glow are the ones that are live, selected, or asking for help.

  1. 01The trail means "here" or "live"The peach→pink gradient marks where you are and what's streaming right now. It never decorates.
  2. 02Peach speaks, pink switchesAnything with words on it uses peach: peach text on its own, white text on a peach wash. Pink is only for wordless state like checks, switches and tab underlines.
  3. 03Dense, but never crampedTables run at 36px rows and a 13px base. Weight and colour carry hierarchy so size can stay small.
01

Brand

The wordmark from the app, redrawn for night: "STEP" and the emblem's shadow layers take the ink colour, and "WHERE" keeps the brand gradient. When the sidebar collapses, only the emblem shows.

Wordmark · expanded sidebar, sign-in
Emblem · collapsed sidebar, favicon

The trail

One gradient, two stops, left to right.

#F5CF8D peach#FF66CB pink

Use the trail for

  • The active sidebar item's edge line
  • The selected table row's edge line
  • Live and realtime indicators
  • Keyboard focus, as a hairline with a soft halo
  • Brand moments: the wordmark, sign-in, empty states

Never use it for

  • Button fills or other click targets (peach does that job)
  • Backgrounds, cards or section headers
  • Text other than the wordmark
  • Status: it never means "ok" or "warning"
  • More than one moving trail element per view
02

Neutral near-black with no blue or grey tint. Surfaces change only in lightness, a few points per step. Peach and pink together cover about a tenth of any screen, and status colours appear only where state lives.

Space, corners, depth, motion

A 4px grid, compact controls and crisp corners. Depth comes from borders and surface steps; shadows appear only on overlays, where they read as lift.

Corners: crisp and concentric

When one element sits inside another, the outer radius is the inner radius plus the padding: an 8px panel with 2px padding holds 6px controls.

On near-black, drop shadows only register under something floating. Everything else is separated by a 1px line and one surface step.

Motion: felt, not watched

  • Press120msscale 0.97, ease-out
  • Hover, colour160msbackground and colour only
  • Menu, tooltip160msfades in from 0.96 scale at the trigger
  • Dialog, sheet200msfades in from 0.96 scale, centred
  • Live breathe2.4sthe only looping animation; stops with reduced motion
  • Command palette0msused a hundred times a day, so it opens instantly

Ease-out cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1). Only transform and opacity animate, and never transition: all.
