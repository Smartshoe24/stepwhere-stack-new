Stepwhere billing with Cashfree autopay

Our own screens from start to finish. Cashfree only shows its checkout and the user's UPI app or bank approves the autopay. Built from the app's theme tokens and Profile cards. Example family: Priya pays; Riya and Arjun are on their free month; Kabir's shoe is added later. Today is 14 Nov.

What changed from the App Store version
  • Nothing is charged while free days remain. People set up autopay now, and the first charge happens when the earliest selected shoe's free month ends. Other shoes' leftover free days come off that first charge, so nobody loses days.
  • Exact prices. ₹397, ₹549 and ₹899 per shoe, times the number of shoes. No store price points or rounding.
  • New "Review plan" screen with a dated list of every charge before anyone pays.
  • Adding a shoe costs only the rest of the period, charged when that shoe's own free month ends.
  • Cancel, payment method and invoices live in the app. No "Manage in App Store" or "Restore purchases".
  • New states: waiting for bank approval, autopay turned off from the UPI app, charge scheduled, and GST invoices.
  • Unchanged: anyone in the family can buy; only the person who paid can change or cancel it; 3-day grace; everything locks after it.
